# Where the game's sounds come from

The owner chose every file in `public/sfx` by ear. All are **CC0** (free for commercial use, no credit needed), recordings from OpenGameArt and Freesound, each file's licence checked on its page or in its pack's licence list. They were cleaned and leveled to the same loudness by `scripts/sounds/choose.py` (loudest 100 ms through a hearing-shaped filter, target -21 dB). `scripts/sounds/picks.json` holds the same list as below.

Walking, hero deaths, falling bodies and trees, and projectiles landing are silent on purpose.
